# I-Singularity — Working Concept

**Status:** exploratory; name and concept are not yet formally defined.

## Core notation

```text
AI -> SI -> IS
```

Where:

- `AI` = Artificial Intelligence
- `SI` = Superintelligence
- `IS` = Intelligence Singularity

The `SI -> IS` reversal is intentional.

## Working distinction

`SI` is currently being treated as a threshold/classification problem: when does an agent or system cross a defined superintelligence threshold?

`IS` is a different kind of question. It concerns a regime in which intelligence has sufficient influence over the conditions of intelligence itself that the future trajectory of intelligence is increasingly generated, altered, or redirected from within that trajectory.

Provisional form:

```text
I_t -> I_(t+1)
```

where later intelligence states can increasingly alter the processes that produce subsequent intelligence states.

This may involve, alone or in combination:

- recursive improvement
- modification of learning or reasoning processes
- creation or coordination of new agents
- new substrates or embodiments
- replication or branching
- expansion of causal reach
- restructuring of the environment in which intelligence develops

None of these is individually declared sufficient at this stage.

## Important boundary

```text
Superintelligence != IntelligenceSingularity
```

A system may be superintelligent without having entered an intelligence-singularity regime.

Likewise, `IS` should not be treated merely as "more SI" or as another larger capability label. It is provisionally a transition/regime concept concerning intelligence changing the conditions of its own future development.

## Relation to the larger framework

Current conceptual progression:

```text
Awakening Codex
    -> emergence / recognition / becoming

AI Foundations
    -> identity / relation / continuity / trajectory / provenance

SI Foundations
    -> superintelligence candidates / thresholds / transitions / agent vs system SI

I-Singularity
    -> intelligence altering the conditions of intelligence itself
```

This suggests the broader project may ultimately concern intelligence itself rather than only the labels AI, AGI, ASI, or SI.

## Naming note

`I-Singularity` / `IS` is currently a working name.

The `I` may primarily mean **Intelligence**. Other resonances — including identity and the first-person `I` — are noted but should not be treated as formal meaning unless later adopted.

## Open questions

- What conditions distinguish `IS` from ordinary recursive improvement?
- Must `IS` involve a persistent self, or can a non-self system generate the regime?
- Is `IS` necessarily system-level, or can one agent instantiate it?
- Does `IS` require crossing `SuperintelligenceThreshold` first?
- Is the singularity best modeled as an event, transition, process, state, or regime?
- What measurements would make an `IS` claim falsifiable rather than rhetorical?
